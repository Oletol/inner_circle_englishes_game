How to read the cards

Words in capitals (TRAP, BATH, GOAT, FACE and others) are J. Wells’s lexical sets. Each one names the vowel of that keyword and of all words with the same vowel, e.g. GOAT is the vowel of go, boat, know. Each card compares the accent with RP (or with GA). Transcriptions in square brackets show the typical pronunciation.

  • Click or tap a card to open its description, then click an accent.
  • With a mouse, you can also drag the card onto an accent.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a card, Enter to choose it, 1–8 to place it, Esc to cancel.
  • After two mistakes on the same card you can ask for a hint.
  • Sorted cards stay in the accent zones. Open any of them to review the explanation. The game is saved in this browser.
